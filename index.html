    Brain Explorer

    Turn a 3D model of the human brain, zoom in, and tap any part to see what it does.

    • ☝Drag to rotate. Pinch to zoom in and out, and drag with two fingers to move the model. With a mouse, scroll to zoom and right-drag (or Shift-drag) to move.
    • 🧠Tap a colored part or a label, or pick one from the parts menu at the top.
    • 👁Switch on X-ray to find structures hidden deep inside, or Meninges to see the brain’s protective layers.
    • ⚡Tap Nervous system to explore nerves through the whole body, or Sections to see how the system is organized.
    • 🦴Open Spinal cord for a close-up of the cord’s inside, its pathways and its levels.
    • 🧬Open Diseases to watch how Alzheimer’s, Parkinson’s and migraine affect the brain and nerves.
    • 👂Open Senses of the mind to see how each sense is detected, how its signals reach the brain, and how the brain makes sense of them.
    • 💡Open Thinking organs of the mind to look closely at the cerebrum, the six layers of the neocortex and the cerebellum, and see how they work.
    • 🔌Open The hardware of the mind to see a neuron up close, dendrites at work in a network, grey and white matter, and how the whole brain communicates.
    • 🌙Open Sleep and the brain to play through a night of sleep stage by stage, and see the circuits that switch the brain between waking and sleeping.
    • ❤️Show or hide the blood vessels and their flow with the buttons on the right.
